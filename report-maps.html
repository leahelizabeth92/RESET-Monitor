Suspicious-person & drug reports in San Francisco: concentrated or expanded?

Each map year runs October through September, so the latest year (Oct 2025 – Sep 2026) is a full twelve months. Calls and incidents are counted in hexagons roughly 2–3 blocks across (about 10 acres).

Short answer: resident calls both concentrated and shifted south and west

  • Resident 911 calls about suspicious persons are back to 2019 volume, in fewer places. Calls fell from 28,098 a year to about 17,500 in 2023–24, then rebounded to 27,955. In 2019, 80% of them were spread over 367 hexagons; now it takes 268.
  • The Mission absorbed most of the rebound. Mission calls went from 3,434 (2019) to 2,131 (2024) to 6,064 now, close to the Tenderloin's 6,758. Of the roughly 10,400 calls added citywide since 2024, 38% were in the Mission.
  • Nob Hill and Hayes Valley are the other growth areas. Nob Hill more than doubled since 2019 (813 → 1,771) and Hayes Valley rose from 863 to 1,224, both on the edges of the Tenderloin.
  • SoMa, the Tenderloin and the Castro are below 2019. SoMa is down 19% (4,395 → 3,564), the Tenderloin 5% (7,128 → 6,758) and Castro/Upper Market 59% (1,561 → 643), though SoMa and the Tenderloin have both risen since 2024.
  • Enforcement measures concentrated more sharply. Drug incidents and officer-initiated stops are available below for comparison, but they mostly track where police are working.

Reading these with care. Drug incidents (SFPD incident reports, subcategory "Drug Violation", counted once per incident) and officer-initiated stops mostly measure where police are working, so a jump can reflect enforcement focus as much as street conditions. Resident 911 calls (calls from the public, not started by an officer) are the closest thing to resident reports. Call locations are snapped to the nearest intersection; cancelled and duplicate calls are excluded. Officer-initiated includes street-team (HSOC) entries. Neighborhoods are the city's Analysis Neighborhoods. The RESET Center opened in SoMa in May 2026, inside the latest year. Source: DataSF (datasets wg3w-h783 and 2zdj-bwza), downloaded Oct 4, 2026.
